What is Epsilon?

No polished presentation. There's no curated version of anything. No final result to show. No team behind making everything look polished. There's one person. One idea. A process. The will to make it real.

Faceless by decision, not by excuse. Epsilon is faceless because that allows documenting the process without relying on a unique personal element. The voice, the style, the honesty — that's enough. And it allows the brand to scale without depending on a visible person.

The central question: how to be in the digital world and leave something that helps in the physical one? Not as a speech. As something that works. If everything we do in the digital doesn't connect with something tangible in the physical, we stay on the surface.

Epsilon tries to be that bridge. It doesn't promise it's already working. It promises to show it while it's being built, with the honesty of someone in the process.

The principles guiding this:

  • No filters — I don't present a curated version. I show what happens, including errors and doubts.
  • No smoke — what I say is what I do or what I think. I don't promise what I can't deliver.
  • In public — what I build, I show. Transparency is part of the process.
  • Human, conscious — I'm a software engineer, but I'm also in the process. I'm not code.
  • Interact with people — this is not a monologue. Epsilon seeks real connection.
  • Useful, not performative — if something doesn't work, I don't show it as if it did.
  • Scalable, not urgent — this is long term. It's not a speed race.

I will document: raw process, reflections on building in this era, the life behind the project. No fixed publishing calendar. Real presence when there's something to tell.

The jump yet to come: one idea in motion. Something being refined. The jump hasn't happened yet. And I'll show it while it happens.

Real interaction: this is not a monologue. If what I'm building resonates with you, there's room for connection. It's not "follow me and I'll give you value". It's "if this resonates, we're in a space where connection can happen".

For whom: I'm not looking for everyone. I'm looking for someone who understands what it means to start from zero without team or investors, validates the honesty of an unfiltered process, knows that "building in public" is not a buzzword, and is interested in what AI can contribute without being the protagonist.

Epsilon is under construction.
No team. No investors. No filters.
Just one idea, one will, and the real process of making it real.
