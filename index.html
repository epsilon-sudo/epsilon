Who is Epsilon

Not an entrepreneur showing results.
Someone in the construction.

Epsilon (Ε, ε) — the fifth letter of the Greek alphabet. In mathematics and science, epsilon represents something small but significant: a quantity nearly zero, but with effect.

That's where this starts. From the minimum. With intention to grow.

There's too much noise from entrepreneurs showing polished results while hiding the process. Epsilon covers the opposite space: the process in raw form — what works, what doesn't, the decisions, the errors, the rhythm of building something alone from scratch.

No team. No investors. No filters. Just the reality of trying.

Three pillars

The content is structured around three axes.

01
Raw process
What's being built, the decisions made, the errors found, what works and what doesn't. Attempts, not expertise.
02
Reflections on building now
Thoughts on what it costs, what's harder than it looks, what starting from zero in this era actually asks of you. Honest uncertainty.
03
Life behind the project
The rhythm, the isolation, the impulse to keep going when there's no team to lean on. What it means to build something alone.
